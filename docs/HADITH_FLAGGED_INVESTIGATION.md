## Method

Each record was compared with the entry for **the same book and the same number** that Dorar quotes
under «أصول الحديث» on the hadith's page (the "book text"). Where that left a question, Dorar's
displayed text for the same book and number (the "summary") was read as a second, weaker witness —
weaker because a summary's wording can follow another book. Words are quoted below from our stored
text and from Dorar's pages as read on 2026-10-02.

I could not consult a printed edition or a second independent site, so where Dorar's two texts
disagree with each other the record is treated as unresolved.

Categories: **spelling/formatting** · **shortened reference page** · **real wording difference** ·
**still unclear**.

## Short summary

**Sahih al-Bukhari.** 12 records sampled; all 12 numbers agree with Dorar. 8 have no word
difference from the book text, 3 differ only in spelling (سموات/سماوات twice, ابن/بن once), and 1
(`bukhari:2075`) has a real wording difference: six extra words in our text. That one is held.

**Sahih Muslim.** 12 records sampled; all 12 numbers agree with Dorar. 6 have no word difference
from the book text and 1 differs only in spelling. 5 are unresolved and held: 2 real wording
differences, 2 unclear, and 1 where Dorar has no Muslim book text to compare. In the four where a
word differs, Dorar's own summary for the same number agrees with our text and disagrees with
Dorar's book-text entry, so the open question is which of them matches the printed book.

The sample is 12 of 6,942 eligible Bukhari records and 12 of 7,174 eligible Muslim records. It
cannot show that the remaining records are free of differences like these.
